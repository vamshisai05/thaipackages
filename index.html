Complete 5N/6D Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Suvarnabhumi Airport Private Pickup → Transfer to Premium Sea-View Pattaya Condo → Evening at leisure

Meet your private chauffeur directly at Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport. Enjoy a comfortable private AC drive to Pattaya. Check into your Premium Sea-View Condo with kitchen amenities. Evening at leisure to stroll Pattaya Beach or relax.

Day 2

Coral Island (Koh Larn) Speedboat Tour with Indian Lunch → Alcazar Cabaret Show (VIP Seating)

Morning speedboat excursion to Coral Island (Koh Larn) with crystal-clear waters and white sands, followed by a delicious Indian lunch. In the evening, enjoy VIP seating at the world-famous Alcazar Cabaret Show.

Day 3

Pattaya City Highlights (Big Buddha & coastal viewpoints) → Free evening for night markets

Visit the towering Big Buddha (Wat Phra Yai) and panoramic coastal viewpoints overlooking Pattaya Bay. Spend the afternoon exploring local cafes or relaxing in your condo pool, followed by vibrant evening night markets.

Day 4

Private Checkout Transfer to Bangkok → Check into City-Center Luxury Condo → Shopping/Leisure

Private door-to-door transfer from Pattaya to Bangkok. Check into your City-Center Luxury Condo. Afternoon at leisure for street shopping, visiting Platinum Fashion Mall, or exploring downtown Sukhumvit.

Day 5

Shopping at Pratunam/MBK → Evening Chao Phraya Princess Luxury Dinner Cruise with live music

Enjoy shopping at Pratunam, MBK Center, or ICONSIAM. In the evening, board the Chao Phraya Princess Luxury Dinner Cruise with live music, breathtaking illuminated views of Wat Arun and the Grand Palace, and a lavish buffet.

Day 6

Relaxed breakfast → Private Transfer to Bangkok Airport → Flight home

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast in your condo or partner cafe. Your private AC chauffeur will transfer you directly to Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang Airport for your return flight home.
