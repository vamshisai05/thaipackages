Free Thailand Digital Arrival Card Guide

The Kingdom of Thailand has modernized entry documentation with an online electronic arrival card. This digital submission replaces the traditional paper TM6 card, allowing you to breeze through airport immigration in minutes. It is 100% free to complete.

✓ 100% Free Official Submission ✓ No Third-Party Fees ⏱ Takes Only 3 Minutes
🛡️

100% Free Government Process - Beware of Fee-Charging Scams

The Thai Digital Arrival Card is completely free of charge. Never pay fees on unauthorized third-party websites claiming to process arrival cards for $20 to $50. If you book any ThaiPackages holiday, our dedicated concierge team assists you in completing this form at zero additional cost.

STEP-BY-STEP SUBMISSION

How to Complete Your Digital Arrival Card in 4 Steps

Submit your details online within 72 hours before your scheduled flight arrival in Thailand.

1

Passport & Personal Info

Enter your basic traveler details matching your passport bio page.

  • Full name as per passport.
  • Passport number & expiry date.
  • Nationality & Date of Birth.
  • Contact phone & email ID.
2

Flight & Travel Schedule

Provide your inbound international flight information.

  • Arrival Date & Time in Thailand.
  • Inbound Flight Number (e.g., TG330, AI332, 6E1053).
  • Port of Entry (BKK, DMK, or HKT).
  • Purpose of Visit: Holiday / Tourism.
3

Accommodation Address

Enter your first night hotel or condo address in Thailand.

  • Select Province (Bangkok / Chonburi-Pattaya / Phuket).
  • Enter Condo / Hotel Name.
  • Full Street Address & Postal Code.
  • (See address helper below).
4

Save Digital QR Code

Download and screenshot your digital confirmation.

  • Instant submission confirmation.
  • Save QR code on your mobile phone.
  • No mandatory paper printout needed.
  • Show QR code at immigration if requested.

HELPFUL INFORMATION

Digital Arrival Card FAQs

Everything you need to know about the digital entry form.

When is the best time to fill out the digital arrival card?

We recommend filling it out 24 to 72 hours before your flight departure. This ensures your flight seat and accommodation details are finalized.

Do I need a physical printout of the arrival card?

No physical printout is required. Thai Immigration scanners can scan the digital QR code directly from your smartphone screen. However, saving a screenshot in your phone gallery is recommended in case of offline airport Wi-Fi.

What if my flight number or hotel changes after submission?

You can either resubmit a new digital form with your updated flight or hotel details, or simply inform the immigration officer at the counter during entry clearance.

Does every family member need an individual arrival card?

Yes. Each traveler holding an individual passport (including children and infants) must have an electronic arrival card submission linked to their passport number.
